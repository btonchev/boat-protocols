# Land To Water

## Deployment sequence

1. Secure hitch in place
1. Mount plugs
1. Remove the side windshields 
1. Open gasoline breather 
1. Load all equipment on the boat
1. ⁠Check we have water, sunscreen, etc.
1. Disconnect photovoltaic land panel
1. Connect photovoltaic boat panel
1. Install the camera
1. Turn on batteries fuses
1. Turn on chart ploter
1. Check all system up-to-date and status green 
1. Trim up the m⁠otor
1. Put Bob Marley at the speaker...😅🤟🏻🥳

## Teardown sequence

1. Wash the trolling motor lock mechanism
1. Wash the rexroth profile joint
1. Wash the motor with the motor flusher
1. Mount plugs
1. Trim up the m⁠otor
1. Close gasoline breather
1. Connect photovoltaic land panel
1. Disconnect photovoltaic boat panel
1. Turn off batteries fuses
1. Remove the camera
1. Unload all equipment of the boat
